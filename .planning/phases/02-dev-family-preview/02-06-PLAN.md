## Phase Goal

**As a** family product owner, **I want to** use a safe Family Preview of our week on my phone, **so that** I can try changes without risking Home.

<objective>
Slice 6: Preview as she will use it, run by three commands. `deploy-preview <branch>` puts any branch on Preview, stamps its version line and proves the new cards are really being served. `reset-preview` brings back the original pretend week with nothing old resurrected. `refresh-preview` keeps the week current without wiping. Dev gets the same reset as `dev-reset`. The event sync from plan 02-05 is wired into seed and refresh here.

Purpose: PREV-01 (separate instance, compose side), PREV-05, PREV-06, PREV-07 (deploy side), DEV-03 (reset), and the hand-off note ACCESS-05 relies on.
Output: dev/compose.preview.yaml, scripts deploy-preview, reset-preview, dev-reset, refresh-preview, preview-down, lib.sh additions, tools/seed/deploy.py, the `refresh`, `verify-served` and `note` commands, unit, stub-orchestration and live tests.

Decisions implemented: D-01 (own container, volume, port 8124), D-03 (restart unless-stopped), D-19 (hand-off note), D-20 (stamp substituted and verified after deploy), D-27 (same logins after reset), D-28 (refresh command; the schedule itself is in plan 02-08). The Google backup and sign-in preservation hooks are added to these scripts by plan 02-07.
</objective>
