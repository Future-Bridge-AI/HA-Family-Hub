## Phase Goal

**As a** family product owner, **I want to** use a safe Family Preview of our week on my phone, **so that** I can try changes without risking Home.

<objective>
Slice 2: two small, additive changes to the served calendar card, so she can always tell which version she is looking at and cannot delete a real family event by accident in Dev or Preview. (1) A build stamp footer (D-20, UI-SPEC S3) whose three tokens deploy-preview fills in later (plan 02-06). (2) A second, loud confirm before deleting an event on a Google calendar in Dev or Preview (D-09, UI-SPEC S4), keyed on HA's location name, which the seed sets to "Family Hub DEV" / "Family Hub PREVIEW" and which Home never uses.

Purpose: PREV-07 and the D-09 guardrail; also the version line ACCESS-05 relies on.
Output: edits to `custom_components/family_hub/card/family-week-calendar-card.js` only (the served copy) and a node check script.

Decisions implemented: D-09 (mechanism, at the planner's discretion: HA location name plus the delete-support platform, no backend or settings-schema change), D-17 (no banner strip; identity by name, theme and stamp), D-20 (stamp text and console.info). UI-SPEC A4 is followed: both strings are English literals with no new translation keys, so the 2,139-key parity holds. UI-SPEC A6 stays as stated: add and edit are not guarded (flagged in plan 02-09's guide and in the threat model). Code-health constraint: the changes are additive helpers plus two small hooks; nothing in the monolith is restructured.
</objective>
