## Phase Goal

**As a** family product owner, **I want to** use a safe Family Preview of our week on my phone, **so that** I can try changes without risking Home.

<objective>
Slice 7: Preview's calendar shows the family's real week from the chosen Google calendars, behind the guardrails the developer asked for. The Google sign-in survives a reset (D-10). Only the chosen calendars are enabled. Pretend events go only to writable chosen calendars.
